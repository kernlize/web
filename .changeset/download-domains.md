The `torrent`, `nzb` and `soulseek` domains over programs the person runs, through which an applet can add torrents and NZBs, follow and control their jobs, read a torrent's metadata and change its limits and sharing, and search and browse shared libraries: `createTorrentService`, `createNzbService`, `createSoulseekService`, `createDownloadClients` (`kernl.downloads`) and `createKernlDownloads`. A person may keep any number of connections, several to one program among them (`DownloadConnection`, with a random `id` and a `label`): `check`, `add`, `update`, `remove`, `chooseDefault` and `routing`. kernl sends a new torrent to the connection that already holds it, else the domain's default, sends searches to the default, and keeps each job and query on its connection; NZB applets choose among connections by label. Secrets are kept under `download:<connection id>:password` and `…:apiKey`. New reasons `invalid:label` and `invalid:connection`. The domains are offered only when the installed `@kehto/shell` prelude renders a binding table (`supportsDomainBindings`), or `KernlOptions.downloads.offer` says the host's own does. Passwords and keys go to a `SecretsProvider` the host can replace through `KernlOptions.secrets`; `createStorageSecrets` is the default and `createMemorySecrets` keeps them for the session only.

**Breaking.** `ConsentSummary.event` is optional: a download question is not about an event, so a `presentConsent` implementation must not assume one. `Kernl` gains the required members `downloads` and `secrets`, so anything that implements the interface itself must supply both.
