The mailboxes are a module, `mailboxesModule()`, in `kernlModules()`. It sets the `outbox` tier from the identity's relay list and signs its save with the active signer; without it the `outbox` tier stays as set. `KernelContext` gains `signer()`, for writes the host starts for the person (D-065), and `find(id)`. `KernlNetwork` has no `mailboxes`, and `KernlNetworkParts` no `directory`.
