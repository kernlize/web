Typed asks (D-067). `KernlHost.asks` takes one renderer per kind of question the kernel puts to the person: `sign.replace`, `upload.file`, `download.add`, `connection.direct`, `firewall.approve`, `intent.choose-handler`, `messaging.choose-reply` and `settings.present`. Each `Ask` carries its facts and kernl's own words (`defaultText`). A kind nobody draws, a renderer that fails, and an answer for a window that has closed or runs another applet get the kind's refusal, never an allow; only the firewall's question is timed out. `presentConsent`, `presentApproval`, `chooseHandler`, `chooseReply` and `presentSettings` still draw the kinds they used to and are deprecated; `presentConsent` is now optional. `createAskChannel`, `toAskChannel`, `ASK_KINDS` and `ASK_REFUSALS` are exported. Breaking for module authors: `KernelContext.ask(summary)` is now `ask(ask)`, for the kinds a module lists in `KernlModule.asks`, and rejects `invalid:ask-undeclared:<kind>` otherwise; `KernelContext.host` loses the prompt callbacks, and `canAsk(kind)` is new. `createSigningConsent`, `createConsentHandler`, `askUploadConsent` (which gains `windowId`) and `createFirewallPermissions` (which gains `asks`) take a host or an ask channel. `createKernlDownloads` takes `ask`, and `createKernlIntents` takes `choose`. `createDirectConnectionsService`'s `ask` receives the ask. `settingsModule()` without a form draws through the host's `settings.present` renderer, and `createKernl` no longer forwards `presentSettings` itself.
